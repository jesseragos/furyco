Our Story

More Than
a Gym

Certified S&C 500+ Athletes 12 Sports Custom Builds

Fury Co. Athletics started in 2018 with a simple belief: athletes deserve coaching built around them, not a one-size-fits-all routine. We design periodized programs that target the specific demands of your sport and your athlete profile — and we hold you to the standard that gets results.

Today the Fury team coaches more than 500 athletes across 12 sports, from youth development through collegiate and professional performance. We also design and install custom gym build-outs — home gyms to full commercial facilities — bringing the same precision to the space as we do to the training.
